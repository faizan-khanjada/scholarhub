Our Story

From a Student Project to a Global Platform

ScholarHub began in 2026 as a simple idea in a university dorm room. Our founders realized that while millions of dollars in scholarships went unclaimed every year, students were struggling to find funding simply because the information was scattered and inaccessible.

What started as a shared spreadsheet among friends has grown into a comprehensive platform connecting thousands of students with life-changing opportunities.

Today, we're proud to serve students from over 50 countries, partnering with leading universities and organizations to make education accessible to everyone.
